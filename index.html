📖 What Makes an Ingredient "Clean" or "Toxic"?

In the app interface, we display simplified labels: "Clean" (for Generally Clean ingredients) and "Toxic" (for Potentially Toxic ingredients). Here's what that means:

Our Philosophy: Health Freak analyzes ingredients through a "crunchy lifestyle" lens - prioritizing whole, organic, unprocessed foods while being cautious about synthetic additives, artificial ingredients, and heavily processed substances. Our AI is specifically prompted to evaluate ingredients from this wellness-focused perspective, which may be more conservative than mainstream regulatory standards.

Health Freak's AI analyzes ingredients based on:

  • Scientific research on health effects and potential concerns
  • Natural vs. synthetic origins - preferring minimally processed, naturally-derived ingredients
  • Common allergens and sensitivities
  • Processing methods and artificial additives
  • Holistic health perspective - considering ingredients that may be "generally recognized as safe" but still flagged based on wellness principles
Important Note: Our classifications represent educational opinions from a holistic wellness perspective, not medical advice or regulatory determinations. An ingredient marked "Potentially Toxic" may still be approved by regulatory agencies - we simply approach food through a more cautious, natural-focused lens. Always consult with healthcare professionals for personalized dietary guidance.
